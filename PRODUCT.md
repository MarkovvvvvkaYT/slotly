# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Customers choose a trusted local specialist and a convenient time without messaging. Independent specialists create a public booking page, manage services and working hours, and receive bookings in one workspace.

## Product Purpose

Slotly turns a specialist's availability into a simple, reliable booking experience. Success means a visitor can discover a fitting service, see its terms, choose an available slot, and create a booking with no ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, precise, optimistic.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI SaaS dashboards, neon/cyberpunk, decorative glass panels, noisy 3D, oversized pills, and category-specific salon imagery. The product must feel useful for any independent specialist.

## Design Principles

- Make time feel tangible: slots, calendars, and availability should be legible at a glance.
- Let the booking path stay calm and direct.
- Use depth to explain hierarchy, never as decoration.
- Keep customer discovery warm and human; keep specialist workspaces disciplined and efficient.
- Every visible control must correspond to a real product action or state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast where practical, retain visible focus states and keyboard paths, use semantic form controls, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
